About Us

Our Story

We cater to the domestic travel enthusiasts in Kerala, mainly focusing on north Kerala who wishes to travel to places abroad or to unexplored destinations in Malabar region. Our main packages include All Kerala tour packages, North Kerala packages, South Kerala packages, hills, beaches and backwater packages, and family and group holiday packages. We are committed to offering travel services of the highest quality, combining our energy and enthusiasm, with our years of experience. Our greatest satisfaction comes in serving large numbers of satisfied clients who have experienced the joys and inspiration of travel. Knowledge of our field paired with excellence in customer service is our mantra. Our goal is to exceed your expectations every time with outstanding service. We are led by an experienced management team. We will be there for you if anything unexpected occurs on your trip. We have the education and the experience to ensure you enjoy exceptional travel experiences throughout your lifetime. “when traveling with us & explore the other sides of gods own country”.
