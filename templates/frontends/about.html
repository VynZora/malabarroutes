Our Story

Kerala Best Travel Agency for Exploring North and South Kerala

A Story That Begins in Malabar

Malabar Routes started with a focus on North Kerala and its distinctive cultural identity. The region has plenty to offer beyond its familiar stops, from historic landmarks and local traditions to quieter destinations that reward a little more time.

As the business developed, its travel offering expanded beyond Malabar to cover destinations across Kerala. Today, travellers can enquire about trips across the northern and southern regions of the state, with options suited to different travel interests. That background continues to shape our approach to planning, communication and helping travellers understand their arrangements before booking.

We know that a holiday can involve several moving parts. An itinerary may look straightforward on paper, but travel times, arrival schedules and the distance between destinations can make a difference to the experience. We encourage travellers to discuss these details before confirming their plans.

For people comparing kerala tour companies, the details matter more than the promises. Understanding the proposed itinerary, the responsibilities of the travel provider and the booking conditions gives you a clearer basis for choosing a company that suits your needs.

Malabar Routes brings its regional roots to a wider range of destinations, helping travellers discover more of Kerala with a clearer idea of what their trip involves.
